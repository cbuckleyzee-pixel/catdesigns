What is Debrief?

Users create private groups for events, record moments as they happen, and see everyone’s shared content the next day in a group “debrief.” The app has been beta tested, and work continues on UI/UX clarity, a simpler core flow, and App Store readiness.

What we focused on

  • Aligning the overall design.
  • Keeping a consistent user flow.
  • Differentiating from existing solutions.
  • Simplifying the experience.

Empathizing with our user

We mapped the user journey across five stages: Awareness, Consideration, Download, Service, and Loyalty. The middle stages had the most pain points: an unclear UI, confusion about the locking feature, and poor integration with tools people already use. Engagement also dropped once the initial novelty wore off.

[User journey map]

A competitor audit compared Debrief with BeReal and Lapse to help us find where it could stand apart.

What I learned

The main challenge was keeping all of the original app’s functions while redesigning it. A few things stood out:

  • User research often challenges what we assume.
  • Small navigation changes can have a big effect on usability.
  • Feedback-driven iteration leads to stronger decisions than intuition alone.
